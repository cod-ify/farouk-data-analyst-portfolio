Problems I’d Like to Solve

Good analysis should make work easier, decisions better or problems smaller.

I like figuring out what is actually happening when the headline number does not tell the whole story — then using data, automation or AI to do something useful about it.

01

Finding the real issue

Sometimes a project, supplier or team looks like it is struggling, but the real cause is hidden. I dig beneath the headline metric — workload, case mix, process gaps or one-off events — so the business can fix the cause rather than the symptom.

02

Cleaning up messy tasks

Repeated exports, manual checks and copy-paste reporting waste time and create errors. I want to use SQL, Python, Power Query and AI-assisted workflows to turn repetitive work into cleaner, repeatable processes.

03

Testing what works

Whether it is a new feature, service change or operational intervention, I want to use data to see what people actually use, what improves outcomes and what is worth keeping.

04

Predicting what comes next

Historical data can help teams anticipate demand, delays, cost pressure or risk. I want to use statistical and machine-learning methods where they genuinely improve planning — not simply because they sound impressive.

05

Building useful AI automation

Not every AI problem needs a model from scratch. I want to combine business rules, data and generative AI to automate triage, summarisation, reporting and information retrieval, while keeping human review where it matters.
